Dojo

A visual prototype, so click around.

  • Everything here is mock data, a frozen copy of a sample project my agents worked on.
  • It is not connected to Claude or GitHub. Chat gives a canned reply, nothing is saved, and it all resets when you reload.
  • Open a card in Review, answer it, approve it, or check the office and the team. That is the interface as I use it.

About this project

  • Dojo is my own productivity tool: a pixel office where I ran a team of AI agents.
  • I used it for a couple of months to build a few products, and it lifted my productivity a lot.
  • It is parked now. I am building a brand-new workflow with Claude, with more complex agentic orchestration, and I may come back to refine this one.
